About the experiment

We manipulate speaking rate while holding the synthetic voice and utterance text constant within each pair.

Authority is a listener judgment, not an intrinsic property of slower or faster speech. Clarity and urgency are measured separately.

Ratings are held temporarily in a local Django session, identified by your browser session cookie. Use Export to save them. Reset erases the current session's ratings.
