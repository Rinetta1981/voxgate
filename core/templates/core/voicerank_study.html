Before you begin

This listening activity takes approximately 3–5 minutes. You will hear eight synthesized clips and rate authority, clarity and urgency from 1 (low) to 5 (high).

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time or reset this browser session. Responses and a randomly generated participant code remain in your local Django session until you export the CSV. No name, email address or account is requested; the CSV has no name or IP address. The local development server may still record ordinary request logs.

No data is automatically submitted to a researcher, cloud service, or language model. Exporting a CSV places a copy on your computer; resetting cannot delete copies you already exported.

Before recruiting participants for research, obtain appropriate ethics review and an approved information/consent procedure.
